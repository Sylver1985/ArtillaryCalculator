What the calculator keeps

There are no accounts and nothing you type is sent to us. To save you retyping, your browser remembers your gun position and height, grid scale, weapon and targeting mode (in its own local storage, on your device only). Clear all, or clearing your browser's site data, removes them.

Hosting

Like any website, the site's web host keeps short-lived access logs (IP address, time, page requested) for running and securing the service.

Advertising

The calculator is free and paid for by advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's My Ad Center, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' personalised ads at aboutads.info. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

The LobbyForge ad links to lobbyforge.net with campaign tags in the address so it can count visits from the calculator; they identify the calculator, not you. Paid sponsors' ads link straight to the sponsor's own site.

Donations

Donations are made on PayPal's site and handled entirely by PayPal under its own privacy policy. We receive only what PayPal passes on to anyone you pay, such as your name and the amount.
